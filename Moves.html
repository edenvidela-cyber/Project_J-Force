Move Creator!

Project J-Force's move creation system is made to be flexible and intuitive, allowing players to create their own unique moves with ease.

The Move Creation system allows you to choose from 4 different archetypes: Physical, Energy, Enhancement, and Unique/Other. Physical moves are your standard punches, kicks, and weapon strikes. Energy moves are your classic energy blasts, beams, and projectiles. Enhancement moves are buffs and debuffs that can be applied to yourself or others. Unique/Other moves are for anything that doesn't fit into the other categories, such as transformation or teleportation moves.
