Character Customization

Project J-Force's character customization system is designed to give players the freedom to create their own unique fighters, with a wide range of options for clothes, abilities, and moves. Players can choose from a variety of base characters inspired by popular anime and manga series, or create their own original character from scratch.

The Fighting style system allows you to choose from vanilla character styles, or even modded styles created by the community! Or, you can make your own with bits and pieces of each style!
